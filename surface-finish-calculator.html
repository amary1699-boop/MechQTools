What is Surface Finish?

Surface Finish or Surface Roughness describes the small peaks and valleys present on a manufactured metal surface.

A surface may look smooth to our eyes, but under magnification it contains very small irregularities.

Lower Ra = smoother surface
Higher Ra = rougher surface

Ra क्या है?

Ra = Roughness Average

Ra is the average surface roughness value measured from the microscopic peaks and valleys of a surface.

It is one of the most commonly used surface roughness parameters on mechanical engineering drawings.

Example:
If a drawing says:

Ra 3.2 µm

it means the specified surface roughness requirement is Ra = 3.2 micrometres.

Rz क्या है?

Rz = Roughness Depth Parameter

Rz describes the height difference between surface peaks and valleys over specified sampling lengths.

Simple way to remember:

Ra → Average roughness

Rz → Peak / valley height based roughness parameter

µm क्या होता है?

Micrometre (µm)

Surface roughness values are normally very small, so they are often expressed in micrometres (µm).

1 mm = 1000 µm

Therefore:
3.2 µm = 0.0032 mm

इसलिए जब drawing पर Ra 3.2 µm लिखा होता है, तो यह बहुत छोटी surface irregularity requirement को indicate करता है।

Smooth Surface vs Rough Surface

Lower Ra → Smoother

Example: Ra 0.8 µm

Surface is comparatively smooth and may require fine finishing operations.

Higher Ra → Rougher

Example: Ra 6.3 µm

Surface has comparatively larger microscopic irregularities.

Drawing में Surface Finish कैसे पढ़ें?

Example 1

Drawing पर लिखा है:

Ra 1.6 µm

मतलब उस specified surface की required roughness Ra 1.6 µm है।

Example 2

Drawing पर लिखा है:

Ra 3.2 µm

मतलब specified surface के लिए Ra requirement 3.2 µm है।

Quality Inspection Tip: Drawing में जो surface-finish value दी गई है, inspection के समय उसी specified requirement को follow करें। Calculator का approximate Ra/Rz conversion acceptance criteria को replace नहीं करता।
