Formulas

Total Opportunities = Total Units × Opportunities per Unit

DPU = Total Defects ÷ Total Units

DPMO = Total Defects ÷ Total Opportunities × 1,000,000

Yield % = (1 − Total Defects ÷ Total Opportunities) × 100

Sigma Level = Approximate conversion from DPMO to Sigma Level

What do these terms mean?

DPMO

DPMO means Defects Per Million Opportunities.

It tells us approximately how many defects would occur if there were 1,000,000 possible defect opportunities.

DPU

DPU means Defects Per Unit.

It shows the average number of defects found per unit.

Yield

Yield tells us the percentage of opportunities that were completed without a defect.

Higher yield generally means better process performance.

Sigma Level

Sigma level is a statistical measure commonly used to describe process performance.

Higher Sigma generally means fewer defects and better process performance.

Example

Suppose a factory inspects:

  • 1,000 units
  • 5 defect opportunities per unit
  • 10 total defects

Then:

Total Opportunities = 1,000 × 5 = 5,000

DPMO = 10 ÷ 5,000 × 1,000,000 = 2,000

This means the process has approximately 2,000 defects per million opportunities.

Important: DPMO and Sigma calculations are useful for process performance analysis. The Sigma conversion shown here is an approximate industry-style reference and can vary depending on the convention used. For actual customer requirements, inspection standards, drawing requirements and company procedures should always be followed.
