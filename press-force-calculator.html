Example Calculation

Suppose:

  • Sheet thickness = 2 mm
  • Cutting length = 100 mm
  • Shear strength = 300 MPa
  • Safety factor = 1.2

Basic cutting force:
100 × 2 × 300 = 60,000 N

With safety factor:
60,000 × 1.2 = 72,000 N

Therefore:
Required press force ≈ 72 kN
≈ 7.34 ton-force

Important Note

This calculator gives an approximate cutting/shearing force. Actual press force can vary depending on:
  • Material grade
  • Actual shear strength
  • Punch and die clearance
  • Cutting geometry
  • Progressive die arrangement
  • Number of cutting operations
  • Press type and operating condition
For production tooling, always verify the force with the actual material specification and die/tooling design.
