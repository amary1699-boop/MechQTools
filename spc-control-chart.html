SPC Control Chart

Analyze subgroup measurements with X̄ (X-bar) and R charts to help monitor process stability.

Important: This tool calculates traditional X̄-R control limits for rational subgroups of size 2–10. Enter measurements from the same characteristic and consistent measurement method. Control limits are not the same as specification limits. A control chart alone does not prove that a process is capable.
