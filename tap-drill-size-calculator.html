How Tap Drill Size Works

  • Major Diameter is the nominal outside diameter of the thread.
  • Pitch is the distance between two adjacent thread peaks.
  • The tap drill creates the hole before internal threading.
  • A smaller tap drill produces a higher percentage of thread engagement.
  • A larger tap drill produces a lower percentage of thread engagement.
  • For normal engineering work, the recommended drill size should be selected according to the required thread percentage, material and applicable standard.
Example — M6 × 1.0

Major Diameter = 6.00 mm
Pitch = 1.00 mm
Basic Tap Drill Approximation:
6.00 − 1.00 = 5.00 mm

Recommended approximate tap drill: 5.00 mm
Important:

The calculator gives an approximate engineering value. Actual tap drill selection can depend on:

• Thread standard
• Required percentage of thread
• Material
• Tapping method
• Tap manufacturer recommendation
• Hole tolerance
For production work, always follow the applicable drawing, thread standard or tooling manufacturer's recommendation.
