1. Basic Quality Engineering Questions

Q1. What is Quality?

हिंदी में: Quality का मतलब है product का customer की requirements, drawing specifications और applicable standards के अनुसार होना।

Interview Answer:
Quality is the ability of a product or service to meet customer requirements, specifications and applicable standards consistently.
Q2. What is the difference between Quality Assurance and Quality Control?

Quality Assurance (QA): Process को सही बनाने और defects रोकने पर focus करता है।

Quality Control (QC): Product या process output को inspect करके defects identify करता है।

Interview Answer:
Quality Assurance is process-oriented and focuses on defect prevention, whereas Quality Control is product-oriented and focuses on identifying defects through inspection and testing.
Q3. What is the difference between Inspection and Testing?

Inspection में product की dimensions, appearance और specified characteristics check की जाती हैं। Testing में material या product की performance और properties verify की जाती हैं।

Interview Answer:
Inspection verifies product characteristics against specifications, while testing evaluates product performance or properties using a defined test method.
Q4. What is a Non-Conformity?

जब product, process या documentation किसी specified requirement को पूरा नहीं करता, तो उसे Non-Conformity कहते हैं।

Interview Answer:
A non-conformity is the failure to meet a specified requirement, drawing specification, customer requirement or applicable standard.
Q5. What is the difference between Correction and Corrective Action?

Correction: अभी मिले defect को ठीक करना या defective part को अलग करना।

Corrective Action: Defect के root cause को हटाना ताकि वह दोबारा न हो।

Interview Answer:
Correction fixes the detected non-conformity, whereas corrective action eliminates its root cause to prevent recurrence.
Q6. What is Preventive Action?

Preventive approach में potential problem को होने से पहले identify करके risk कम किया जाता है। Modern ISO 9001 risk-based thinking पर जोर देता है।

Interview Answer:
Preventive action is an action taken to eliminate the cause of a potential non-conformity or reduce the risk of its occurrence.

2. Inspection & Measuring Instruments

Q7. What is a Vernier Caliper?

Vernier Caliper से external, internal और depth dimensions measure कर सकते हैं। इसकी least count instrument के design पर depend करती है; common metric vernier में 0.02 mm हो सकती है।

Interview Answer:
A Vernier Caliper is a precision measuring instrument used to measure external dimensions, internal dimensions and depth.
Q8. What is a Micrometer?

Micrometer एक precision instrument है जिसका उपयोग thickness, outside diameter या inside dimensions measure करने के लिए किया जाता है। Instrument range और type के अनुसार least count बदलती है।

Interview Answer:
A micrometer is a precision measuring instrument used for accurate measurement of small dimensions, such as thickness and diameter.
Q9. What is Least Count?

किसी measuring instrument से पढ़ी जा सकने वाली सबसे छोटी measurement increment को Least Count कहते हैं।

Interview Answer:
Least count is the smallest value that can be measured or read by a measuring instrument.
Q10. What is Calibration?

Instrument की reading को known reference standard से compare करना Calibration है। इससे measurement reliability और traceability सुनिश्चित करने में मदद मिलती है।

Interview Answer:
Calibration is the comparison of an instrument's measurement against a known reference standard to establish its measurement accuracy and traceability.
Q11. What is a CMM?

CMM यानी Coordinate Measuring Machine। इससे probe की सहायता से X, Y और Z coordinates measure करके dimensions, position, profile और geometry inspect की जाती है।

Interview Answer:
A Coordinate Measuring Machine is used to measure the geometric characteristics of a component by collecting coordinate points in three-dimensional space.
Q12. What is the difference between Accuracy and Precision?

Accuracy: Measurement true/reference value के कितने करीब है।

Precision: Repeated measurements आपस में कितनी close हैं।

Interview Answer:
Accuracy indicates closeness to the true value, whereas precision indicates the closeness of repeated measurements to each other.
Q13. What is a First Piece Inspection?

Production शुरू होने पर पहली तैयार component की drawing और inspection plan के अनुसार complete checking करना First Piece Inspection है।

Interview Answer:
First Piece Inspection is the verification of the first manufactured component against all specified drawing and quality requirements before proceeding with regular production.

3. GD&T and Drawing Questions

Q14. What is GD&T?

GD&T यानी Geometric Dimensioning and Tolerancing। यह engineering drawing पर size, form, orientation, location और runout requirements को define करने की standardized language है।

Interview Answer:
GD&T is a symbolic engineering language used to define and control the geometric requirements of a component on an engineering drawing.
Q15. What is a Datum?

Datum एक theoretically exact reference है, जिसे actual part पर datum feature से establish किया जाता है। इसका उपयोग measurement और geometric tolerancing के लिए किया जाता है।

Interview Answer:
A datum is a theoretically exact reference used to establish a coordinate system for measurement and geometric tolerancing.
Q16. What is Flatness?

Flatness एक form tolerance है। यह surface को दो parallel planes के बीच सीमित करती है। Flatness control के लिए datum की आवश्यकता नहीं होती।

Interview Answer:
Flatness is a form tolerance that controls how much a surface may deviate from a perfectly flat condition within two parallel planes.
Q17. What is Perpendicularity?

Perpendicularity एक orientation tolerance है, जो किसी feature को 90° orientation में datum के reference में control करती है।

Interview Answer:
Perpendicularity is an orientation control that specifies how perpendicular a surface, axis or feature must be to a referenced datum.
Q18. What is Position Tolerance?

Position tolerance किसी feature की theoretically exact location के आसपास allowable positional variation control करती है।

Interview Answer:
Position tolerance controls the allowable variation in the location of a feature, such as a hole or pin, relative to its theoretically exact position.
Q19. What is the difference between MMC and LMC?

MMC: Feature of size में सबसे अधिक material condition। Hole के लिए smallest diameter और shaft के लिए largest diameter।

LMC: Feature of size में सबसे कम material condition। Hole के लिए largest diameter और shaft के लिए smallest diameter।

Interview Answer:
Maximum Material Condition is the condition where a feature contains the maximum amount of material, while Least Material Condition is the condition where it contains the minimum amount of material.

4. Quality Core Tools

Q20. What are the 7 Quality Management Principles or 7 QC Tools?

ध्यान दें: 7 QC Tools और Quality Management Principles अलग concepts हैं। Traditional 7 QC Tools हैं:

  • Check Sheet
  • Histogram
  • Pareto Chart
  • Cause and Effect Diagram
  • Control Chart
  • Scatter Diagram
  • Stratification / Flow Chart (कई practical lists में Flow Chart या अन्य tool शामिल किया जाता है)
Interview Answer:
The seven basic QC tools are used to collect, analyze, visualize and control quality-related data to identify and solve process problems.
Q21. What is Pareto Analysis?

Pareto Analysis में defects को frequency या impact के आधार पर rank किया जाता है। इससे major problems पर priority तय करने में मदद मिलती है। 80/20 एक common guideline है, fixed rule नहीं।

Interview Answer:
Pareto Analysis is a prioritization technique used to identify the most significant causes or categories contributing to a problem.
Q22. What is 5 Why Analysis?

Problem का root cause जानने के लिए बार-बार Why पूछा जाता है। हर problem के लिए ठीक पाँच बार Why पूछना जरूरी नहीं है।

Interview Answer:
5 Why Analysis is a root cause analysis technique in which repeated questioning helps identify the underlying cause of a problem.
Q23. What is an 8D Report?

8D एक structured problem-solving method है:

  • D1: Establish the team
  • D2: Describe the problem
  • D3: Develop interim containment actions
  • D4: Identify and verify root causes
  • D5: Choose permanent corrective actions
  • D6: Implement and validate actions
  • D7: Prevent recurrence
  • D8: Recognize the team and close the problem
Interview Answer:
8D is a structured problem-solving methodology used to identify root causes, implement corrective actions and prevent recurrence of quality problems.
Q24. What is APQP?

APQP यानी Advanced Product Quality Planning। यह product development और launch के दौरान quality planning के लिए structured framework है।

Interview Answer:
APQP is a structured approach used to plan and manage product quality activities throughout product development and launch.
Q25. What is PPAP?

PPAP यानी Production Part Approval Process। इसमें supplier production process और part को customer requirements के अनुसार demonstrate करता है। Automotive industry में submission level और customer-specific requirements लागू होते हैं।

Interview Answer:
PPAP is a process used to demonstrate that the supplier understands all customer engineering design records and specifications and can consistently produce parts meeting those requirements.
Q26. What is FMEA?

FMEA यानी Failure Mode and Effects Analysis। इसमें potential failure modes, उनके effects, causes और risk को evaluate किया जाता है।

Interview Answer:
FMEA is a systematic method used to identify potential failure modes, evaluate their effects and causes, and prioritize actions to reduce risk.
Q27. What is MSA and Gauge R&R?

MSA यानी Measurement System Analysis। Gauge R&R measurement system में Repeatability और Reproducibility variation को evaluate करता है।

  • Repeatability: Same operator, same instrument, same part और same conditions में variation.
  • Reproducibility: Different operators के बीच measurement variation.
Interview Answer:
MSA evaluates the measurement system. Gauge R&R studies repeatability and reproducibility to understand measurement variation.
Q28. What is SPC, Cp and Cpk?

SPC यानी Statistical Process Control। Control charts से process behavior monitor किया जाता है।

Cp: Process spread की तुलना specification width से करता है।

Cpk: Process spread और specification के बीच process centering को भी consider करता है।

Interview Answer:
SPC uses statistical methods to monitor process performance. Cp measures potential process capability, while Cpk considers both process spread and centering relative to specification limits.

5. Sheet Metal Quality & Manufacturing Questions

Q29. What is Bend Allowance?

Bend Allowance bend के curved portion की neutral axis पर developed length है।

Formula: BA = (π / 180) × Bend Angle × (R + K × T)

जहाँ R = Inside Radius, T = Thickness और K = K-Factor है।

Interview Answer:
Bend Allowance is the length of the neutral axis material consumed in the curved portion of a sheet metal bend.
Q30. What is Bend Deduction?

Bend Deduction वह value है जिसे formed outside dimensions के total से subtract करके flat blank length calculate की जाती है।

Interview Answer:
Bend Deduction is the amount subtracted from the sum of outside flange dimensions to calculate the flat pattern length.
Q31. What is K-Factor?

K-Factor neutral axis की inside surface से दूरी और sheet thickness का ratio है। इसकी value material, thickness, radius और bending process के अनुसार बदल सकती है।

Interview Answer:
K-Factor is the ratio of the distance of the neutral axis from the inside surface to the total material thickness.
Q32. What is Springback?

Bending के बाद load हटाने पर material elastic recovery के कारण थोड़ा वापस खुलता है। इसे Springback कहते हैं।

Interview Answer:
Springback is the elastic recovery of sheet metal after the bending force is removed, resulting in a change in the final bend angle or radius.
Q33. What is the difference between Punch and Die?

Punch ऊपर का tool है जो sheet को push करता है। Die नीचे का tool है जो sheet को support और form करता है।

Interview Answer:
The punch applies force to form the sheet metal, while the die supports and shapes the material during the bending operation.
Q34. What are common sheet metal defects?
  • Cracks
  • Wrinkles
  • Surface scratches
  • Dimensional variation
  • Springback
  • Twist and distortion
  • Hole position mismatch
  • Burrs and edge defects
  • Tool marks and dents
Interview Answer:
Common sheet metal defects include cracks, wrinkles, scratches, dimensional variation, springback, distortion, burrs and hole position mismatch.
Q35. What is the purpose of a Bumping Operation?

Bumping में कई छोटे bends लगाकर एक बड़ा radius या curved profile बनाया जाता है। हर hit के angle और bend-line spacing से final profile प्रभावित होता है।

Interview Answer:
Bumping is a sheet metal forming method in which multiple small bends are applied at calculated intervals to produce a larger-radius curve.
Q36. How do you inspect a sheet metal bending component?

Inspection में drawing और control plan के अनुसार ये checks किए जा सकते हैं:

  • Material grade and thickness
  • Overall length, width and height
  • Bend angle and inside radius
  • Hole diameter and position
  • Flatness, perpendicularity and profile
  • Visual defects and surface condition
Interview Answer:
I inspect sheet metal components according to the drawing and control plan. I check material, thickness, dimensions, bend angles, radius, hole positions, geometry and visual defects using suitable calibrated instruments and gauges.

6. Practical & Scenario-Based Questions

Q37. If a dimension is out of tolerance, what will you do?

पहले measurement confirm करें, instrument और drawing revision check करें। फिर affected parts identify करके containment करें और root cause investigate करें। Approved corrective action के बाद re-inspection करें।

Interview Answer:
First, I verify the measurement and check the drawing revision and measuring instrument. Then I segregate the affected parts, inform the concerned team, investigate the root cause, implement approved corrective action and verify the results.
Q38. What will you do if production asks you to accept a rejected part?

बिना approval के rejected part accept नहीं करना चाहिए। Specification, evidence और risk के आधार पर authorized quality/customer approval process follow करना चाहिए।

Interview Answer:
I will not accept a non-conforming part without proper authorization. I will verify the specification, document the non-conformity and follow the approved deviation or concession process, if applicable.
Q39. How will you handle a customer complaint?

Complaint को record करें, stock और in-transit material contain करें, problem verify करें, root cause निकालें, corrective action implement करें और effectiveness verify करके customer को evidence के साथ response दें।

Interview Answer:
I will register the complaint, initiate containment, verify the issue, identify the root cause, implement corrective actions and provide evidence of effectiveness to the customer.
Q40. If the same defect occurs repeatedly, what will you do?

Repeated defect के लिए केवल operator को blame नहीं करना चाहिए। Process parameters, machine, tooling, material, method, measurement और environment investigate करें।

Interview Answer:
I will analyze the process using data and root cause analysis tools. I will investigate machine, material, method, manpower, measurement and environmental factors, then implement and verify permanent corrective actions.
Q41. How do you control rejection in production?

Defect data collect करें, Pareto analysis करें, high-risk processes identify करें, first-piece inspection, in-process inspection, process monitoring और corrective actions लागू करें।

Interview Answer:
I control rejection through process monitoring, first-piece and in-process inspection, defect analysis, standardization, operator training and corrective action verification.

7. HR & Personal Interview Questions

Q42. Tell me about yourself.

नीचे एक sample है। अपनी actual qualification, company और experience के अनुसार इसे बदलें।

Sample Answer:
Good morning, sir/madam. My name is Amar Kumar Yadav. I am from Bihar and currently living in Pune. I have completed my B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering. I am working as a Quality Engineer in the sheet metal manufacturing field. My work involves component inspection, drawing interpretation, dimensional checking and quality documentation. I am interested in learning new technologies and improving my technical and professional skills. Thank you.
Q43. What are your strengths?

ऐसी strength बताएं जिसका example आप दे सकें।

Sample Answer:
My strengths are that I am a quick learner, responsible and willing to learn new things. I focus on understanding problems and improving my work.
Q44. What is your weakness?

ऐसी weakness बताएं जिस पर आप सुधार कर रहे हों।

Sample Answer:
Sometimes I trust people easily. I am improving by verifying information, communicating clearly and making decisions based on facts.
Q45. Why should we hire you?
Sample Answer:
You should hire me because I have practical experience in sheet metal quality inspection, I understand engineering drawings and dimensional requirements, and I am willing to learn and contribute to improving product quality.
Q46. Why do you want to change your job?

पुरानी company की बुराई न करें। Growth और learning पर focus रखें।

Sample Answer:
I am looking for better learning opportunities, more technical responsibilities and professional growth. I want to improve my knowledge and contribute to a well-established organization.
Q47. Where do you see yourself in five years?
Sample Answer:
In five years, I want to become a skilled Quality Engineer with strong knowledge of quality systems, customer requirements, problem-solving and process improvement. I also want to take greater responsibilities in the organization.
