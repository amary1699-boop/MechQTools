Prepare this answer in a natural and confident way. Speak clearly and maintain a professional tone.

1. My name is Amar Kumar Yadav.

मेरा नाम अमर कुमार यादव है।

मैं बिहार से हूँ और वर्तमान में पुणे में रहता हूँ।

मेरे काम में Sheet Metal Inspection, Dimensional Checking और Drawing Verification शामिल हैं।

मेरा करियर लक्ष्य अपनी तकनीकी जानकारी बढ़ाना और एक अच्छी कंपनी में Quality Engineer के रूप में आगे बढ़ना है।

Q2. What are your roles and responsibilities as a Quality Engineer?

Answer (English)

My responsibilities include incoming, in-process and final inspection, dimensional checking, drawing verification, maintaining inspection reports, identifying defects and coordinating with production for corrective actions.

मेरी जिम्मेदारी incoming, in-process और final inspection, drawing के अनुसार dimension check करना, inspection reports बनाना और defects मिलने पर production team के साथ सुधार करवाना है।

Q3. Why did you choose Quality Engineering?

Answer (English)

I am interested in manufacturing processes, dimensional accuracy and problem-solving. Quality Engineering gives me an opportunity to improve product quality and customer satisfaction.

मुझे manufacturing processes, dimensional accuracy और problem-solving में रुचि है। Quality Engineering में product quality सुधारने का अवसर मिलता है।

2. Basic Quality Engineering

Q1. What is Quality?

Answer (English)

Quality is the ability of a product or service to meet specified requirements and customer expectations.

Quality का मतलब है कि product या service निर्धारित requirements और customer expectations को पूरा करे।

Q2. What is Quality Control (QC)?

Answer (English)

Quality Control is the process of inspecting products and identifying defects to ensure that they meet specified requirements.

QC में products की inspection करके defects पहचाने जाते हैं और यह सुनिश्चित किया जाता है कि वे निर्धारित requirements के अनुसार हैं।

Q3. What is Quality Assurance (QA)?

Answer (English)

Quality Assurance focuses on improving processes and preventing defects before they occur.

QA का मुख्य उद्देश्य processes को सुधारना और defects होने से पहले उन्हें रोकना है।

Q4. What is the difference between QC and QA?

Answer (English)

  • QC: Focuses on product inspection and defect detection.
  • QA: Focuses on process improvement and defect prevention.

QC product में defects ढूँढता है, जबकि QA process को सुधारकर defects होने से रोकता है।

Q5. What is an NCR?

Answer (English)

NCR stands for Non-Conformance Report. It is used to document a product or process that does not meet specified requirements.

जब कोई part या process drawing अथवा specification के अनुसार नहीं होता, तो उसकी जानकारी NCR में दर्ज की जाती है।

Q6. What is CAPA?

Answer (English)

CAPA stands for Corrective and Preventive Action. Corrective action addresses the cause of an existing problem, while preventive action addresses potential problems.

Corrective Action मौजूदा समस्या के कारण को हटाता है। Preventive Action भविष्य में संभावित समस्याओं को रोकता है।

3. Inspection and Measuring Instruments

Q1. What is the least count of a Vernier Caliper?

Answer (English)

The least count depends on the instrument design. A commonly used metric Vernier Caliper has a least count of 0.02 mm.

Least count instrument के design पर निर्भर करता है। सामान्य रूप से 0.02 mm वाला Vernier Caliper उपयोग होता है।

Q2. What is the least count of a Micrometer?

Answer (English)

A commonly used metric outside micrometer has a least count of 0.01 mm.

सामान्य metric outside micrometer का least count 0.01 mm होता है। Instrument specification जरूर check करनी चाहिए।

Q3. What is a Height Gauge?

Answer (English)

A Height Gauge is a measuring instrument used to measure vertical dimensions and mark reference heights from a datum surface.

Height Gauge का उपयोग किसी reference surface से height मापने और marking करने के लिए होता है।

Q4. What is a CMM?

Answer (English)

CMM stands for Coordinate Measuring Machine. It measures the geometry of a component using coordinate points.

CMM machine coordinate points की मदद से component के dimensions और geometry की जाँच करती है।

Q5. What is Calibration?

Answer (English)

Calibration is the process of comparing a measuring instrument with a known reference standard to determine its measurement error.

Calibration में instrument की reading को certified reference standard से compare करके measurement error जाँचा जाता है।

Q6. What is the difference between Accuracy and Precision?

Answer (English)

  • Accuracy: How close a measurement is to the true or reference value.
  • Precision: How close repeated measurements are to each other.

Accuracy बताती है कि reading सही value के कितनी करीब है। Precision बताती है कि बार-बार measurement लेने पर readings कितनी एक जैसी हैं।

4. GD&T and Engineering Drawing

Q1. What is GD&T?

Answer (English)

GD&T stands for Geometric Dimensioning and Tolerancing. It is a symbolic system used to define allowable variation in the geometry of a component.

GD&T drawing में component की geometry और उसकी allowable variation बताने का एक symbolic system है।

Q2. What is a Datum?

Answer (English)

A Datum is a theoretically exact reference used for measurement, inspection and geometric tolerancing.

Datum एक reference होता है जिसके आधार पर part की position और geometry check की जाती है।

Q3. What is Flatness?

Answer (English)

Flatness controls how much a surface can deviate from a perfectly flat surface. It does not require a datum.

Flatness यह control करती है कि कोई surface कितनी समतल है। इसके लिए datum की आवश्यकता नहीं होती।

Q4. What is Perpendicularity?

Answer (English)

Perpendicularity controls the orientation of a surface or axis at 90 degrees relative to a datum.

Perpendicularity यह check करती है कि कोई surface या axis datum के मुकाबले 90 degree पर है या नहीं।

Q5. What is the difference between First Angle and Third Angle Projection?

Answer (English)

First Angle and Third Angle are two methods of representing a three-dimensional object in a two-dimensional engineering drawing. The relative positions of the views are different in these methods.

First Angle और Third Angle projection में Front, Top और Side views की positions अलग-अलग होती हैं। Drawing का projection symbol देखकर method पहचानना चाहिए।

Q6. What is Tolerance?

Answer (English)

Tolerance is the permissible variation in a dimension specified by the engineering drawing.

Tolerance किसी dimension में स्वीकार्य बदलाव की सीमा होती है।

Example: 50 ± 0.10 mm means the acceptable range is 49.90 mm to 50.10 mm.

5. Quality Core Tools

Q1. What are the 7 QC Tools?

Answer (English)

The seven basic QC tools are commonly used to collect, analyze and understand quality-related data.

  1. Check Sheet
  2. Histogram
  3. Pareto Chart
  4. Cause and Effect Diagram
  5. Scatter Diagram
  6. Control Chart
  7. Stratification (some lists use a Flow Chart or another variant)

7 QC Tools का उपयोग quality data को समझने, defects का कारण खोजने और process सुधारने के लिए किया जाता है। अलग-अलग references में सातवें tool की सूची अलग हो सकती है।

Q2. What is a Pareto Chart?

Answer (English)

A Pareto Chart is a bar graph arranged in descending order of frequency or impact. It helps identify the most significant problems.

Pareto Chart में सबसे ज्यादा होने वाले defects को पहले दिखाया जाता है, जिससे मुख्य समस्या पर ध्यान दिया जा सके।

Q3. What is 5 Why Analysis?

Answer (English)

5 Why Analysis is a problem-solving method in which we repeatedly ask why to identify the root cause of a problem.

किसी समस्या का असली कारण खोजने के लिए बार-बार Why पूछा जाता है। हर समस्या में ठीक पाँच बार पूछना जरूरी नहीं है।

Q4. What is the 8D Problem Solving Method?

Answer (English)

8D is a structured problem-solving method used to investigate, correct and prevent recurring problems.

  1. D1 – Establish the team
  2. D2 – Describe the problem
  3. D3 – Implement interim containment action
  4. D4 – Identify the root cause
  5. D5 – Select permanent corrective action
  6. D6 – Implement and validate corrective action
  7. D7 – Prevent recurrence
  8. D8 – Recognize the team

8D एक structured method है जिससे problem को समझकर root cause और permanent corrective action निकाला जाता है।

Q5. What is PPAP?

Answer (English)

PPAP stands for Production Part Approval Process. It provides evidence that the production process can consistently meet customer requirements.

PPAP में customer को यह प्रमाण दिया जाता है कि production process लगातार सही part बना सकता है।

Q6. What is APQP?

Answer (English)

APQP stands for Advanced Product Quality Planning. It is a structured approach to planning product and process quality before production.

APQP का उद्देश्य production शुरू होने से पहले product और process quality की planning करना है।

Q7. What is FMEA?

Answer (English)

FMEA stands for Failure Mode and Effects Analysis. It is used to identify potential failure modes, their effects and actions to reduce risk.

FMEA में संभावित failures और उनके effects पहचानकर risk कम करने के लिए actions तय किए जाते हैं।

Q8. What is SPC?

Answer (English)

SPC stands for Statistical Process Control. It uses statistical methods and control charts to monitor process variation.

SPC में statistical methods और control charts की मदद से process variation को monitor किया जाता है।

Q9. What are Cp and Cpk?

Answer (English)

Cp indicates the potential process capability based on process spread, while Cpk also considers how centered the process is within specification limits.

Cp process variation के आधार पर capability बताता है। Cpk यह भी देखता है कि process specification limits के बीच कितना centered है।

Note: Capability indices should be interpreted after checking process stability and data suitability.

Q10. What is MSA and Gauge R&R?

Answer (English)

MSA stands for Measurement System Analysis. Gauge R&R evaluates repeatability and reproducibility of a measurement system.

MSA से measurement system की reliability जाँची जाती है। Gauge R&R में repeatability और reproducibility check करते हैं।

6. Sheet Metal Quality and Manufacturing

Q1. What is Sheet Metal?

Answer (English)

Sheet metal is metal formed into thin and relatively flat sheets. It can be cut, bent, punched and formed into components.

Sheet Metal धातु की पतली sheet होती है जिसे cutting, bending, punching और forming से component बनाया जाता है।

Q2. What is Bend Allowance?

Answer (English)

Bend Allowance is the length of the neutral axis material required for a bend.

Bend Allowance वह material length है जो bend के दौरान neutral axis पर आती है।

Formula:

BA = (π / 180) × Bend Angle × (R + K × T)

  • R = Inside bend radius
  • K = K-Factor
  • T = Material thickness
  • Bend Angle = Bend angle in degrees
Q3. What is Bend Deduction?

Answer (English)

Bend Deduction is the amount subtracted from the sum of outside flange dimensions to calculate the flat pattern length.

Bend Deduction वह value है जिसे outside dimensions के कुल जोड़ से घटाकर flat length निकाली जाती है।

Formula:

BD = 2 × SB - BA

SB = (R + T) × tan(Bend Angle / 2)

Flat Length = Sum of Outside Dimensions - Total Bend Deduction

Q4. What is K-Factor?

Answer (English)

K-Factor is the ratio of the distance from the inside surface to the neutral axis divided by the material thickness.

K-Factor यह बताता है कि material thickness के मुकाबले neutral axis की position कहाँ है।

Formula: K = Neutral Axis Distance / Material Thickness

Q5. What is Springback?

Answer (English)

Springback is the elastic recovery of material after the bending force is removed. It causes a change in the final bend angle or radius.

Bending force हटाने के बाद material थोड़ा वापस अपनी पुरानी position में आता है। इसे Springback कहते हैं।

Q6. What are common sheet metal defects?

Answer (English)

  • Cracks
  • Wrinkles
  • Surface scratches
  • Dimensional variation
  • Excessive springback
  • Distortion
  • Burrs
  • Incorrect bend angle

Sheet Metal में आमतौर पर crack, wrinkle, scratch, dimension variation, springback, distortion और burr जैसे defects आते हैं।

Q7. What is the difference between Punch and Die?

Answer (English)

The punch is the tool that applies force to the sheet, while the die supports or receives the material and helps create the required shape.

Punch material पर force लगाता है और Die material को support देकर required shape बनाने में मदद करता है।

Q8. What is Bumping in Sheet Metal?

Answer (English)

Bumping is a forming method in which a large radius or curved profile is produced through a series of smaller incremental bends.

Bumping में छोटे-छोटे bends देकर धीरे-धीरे बड़ा radius या curved shape बनाया जाता है।

Q9. What will you do if a sheet metal dimension is out of tolerance?

Answer (English)

First, I will verify the measurement and drawing revision. Then I will identify the affected quantity, inform the concerned team, segregate nonconforming parts and support root cause analysis and corrective action.

पहले measurement और drawing revision verify करूँगा। फिर affected parts अलग करूँगा, संबंधित team को बताऊँगा और root cause तथा corrective action में मदद करूँगा।

7. Practical and Situation-Based Questions

Q1. What will you do if production sends a rejected part?

Answer (English)

I will verify the defect, check the drawing and inspection criteria, identify the affected parts, communicate with production and document the nonconformance. I will also support root cause analysis and corrective action.

मैं पहले defect verify करूँगा, drawing और inspection criteria check करूँगा, affected parts अलग करूँगा और production को जानकारी देकर corrective action करवाऊँगा।

Q2. What will you do if two measuring instruments show different readings?

Answer (English)

I will check the calibration status, zero setting, measurement method, contact points and instrument condition. If required, I will verify the result using a suitable calibrated reference instrument.

दोनों instruments का calibration, zero setting, measuring method और condition check करूँगा। जरूरत होने पर calibrated reference instrument से verify करूँगा।

Q3. What will you do if a customer raises a quality complaint?

Answer (English)

I will understand the complaint, verify the issue, identify affected stock and dispatches, take containment action, investigate the root cause and implement corrective and preventive actions.

Customer complaint को समझकर issue verify करूँगा, affected stock की पहचान करूँगा, containment action लूँगा और root cause के अनुसार corrective तथा preventive action करूँगा।

Q4. How do you ensure that a product meets drawing requirements?

Answer (English)

I check the latest drawing revision, dimensions, tolerances, GD&T requirements, material specifications and applicable inspection criteria using suitable calibrated instruments.

Latest drawing revision, dimensions, tolerance, GD&T, material specification और inspection criteria के अनुसार calibrated instruments से part check करता हूँ।

8. HR and Personal Interview Questions

Q1. What are your strengths?

Answer (English)

My strengths are that I am a quick learner, responsible, sincere and willing to improve my technical knowledge. I also try to complete my work carefully.

मेरी ताकत है कि मैं जल्दी सीखता हूँ, जिम्मेदारी से काम करता हूँ और अपनी technical knowledge लगातार बढ़ाने की कोशिश करता हूँ।

Q2. What is your weakness?

Answer (English)

Sometimes I trust people easily. I am improving by verifying information and making decisions based on facts.

कभी-कभी मैं लोगों पर जल्दी भरोसा कर लेता हूँ। अब मैं जानकारी verify करके और facts के आधार पर निर्णय लेने की आदत सुधार रहा हूँ।

Q3. Why should we hire you?

Answer (English)

I have practical experience in quality inspection and sheet metal manufacturing. I understand the importance of drawing requirements, dimensional accuracy and teamwork. I am ready to learn and contribute to your organization.

मुझे quality inspection और sheet metal manufacturing का practical experience है। मैं drawing, dimensional accuracy और teamwork की अहमियत समझता हूँ और कंपनी में योगदान देने के लिए तैयार हूँ।

Q4. Why do you want to change your job?

Answer (English)

I am looking for better learning opportunities, professional growth and the chance to take on more responsibilities in Quality Engineering.

मैं बेहतर learning opportunities, career growth और Quality Engineering में ज्यादा जिम्मेदारी के अवसर की तलाश में हूँ।

Q5. Where do you see yourself in five years?

Answer (English)

In five years, I want to become a skilled Quality Engineer with strong technical knowledge and the ability to handle quality improvement and problem-solving responsibilities.

पाँच साल में मैं एक skilled Quality Engineer बनना चाहता हूँ, जिसे technical knowledge और quality improvement की अच्छी समझ हो।

Q6. What are your hobbies?

Answer (English)

My hobby is learning new things and improving my knowledge.

मेरा शौक नई चीजें सीखना और अपनी जानकारी बढ़ाना है।

Q7. Are you comfortable working in shifts?

Answer (English)

Yes, I understand that manufacturing operations may require shift work. I am comfortable working according to the company's shift requirements.

हाँ, मैं समझता हूँ कि manufacturing में shift के अनुसार काम करना पड़ सकता है। मैं कंपनी की shift requirements के अनुसार काम कर सकता हूँ।
