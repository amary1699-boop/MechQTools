Cp and Cpk Formula

Cp = (USL − LSL) ÷ (6 × σ)

Cpu = (USL − Mean) ÷ (3 × σ)

Cpl = (Mean − LSL) ÷ (3 × σ)

Cpk = Minimum(Cpu, Cpl)

What is Cp?

Cp compares the specification width with the natural process spread. It tells how capable the process could be if it were perfectly centered.

What is Cpk?

Cpk considers both process variation and how close the process mean is to the specification limits.

Important: Cp and Cpk are meaningful when the process is reasonably stable and the standard deviation represents the process variation being evaluated. Always follow your organization's quality standard and customer requirements.

Typical Cpk Interpretation

Cpk Value General Interpretation
Cpk < 1.00 Process is generally not capable.
1.00 ≤ Cpk < 1.33 Marginal / needs improvement.
1.33 ≤ Cpk < 1.67 Generally capable.
1.67 ≤ Cpk < 2.00 Very capable.
Cpk ≥ 2.00 Highly capable.

Example

Suppose a dimension has:

  • USL = 10.05 mm
  • LSL = 9.95 mm
  • Mean = 10.00 mm
  • Standard Deviation = 0.01 mm

Then:

  • Cp = 1.667
  • Cpu = 1.667
  • Cpl = 1.667
  • Cpk = 1.667

The process is generally considered capable at this level.
