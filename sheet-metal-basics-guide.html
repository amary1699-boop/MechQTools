Sheet Metal Basics Guide

Understand sheet metal bending, bend allowance, bend deduction, K-factor, inside radius and flat pattern development with practical examples.

About This Guide

This guide explains the basic concepts used in sheet metal bending and development. It is designed for mechanical engineers, quality engineers, production engineers and sheet metal operators.

1. What is Sheet Metal Bending?

Sheet metal bending is a manufacturing process in which a flat metal sheet is deformed to obtain a required angle or shape. Common machines include press brakes and bending machines.

हिंदी में: Sheet Metal Bending में एक flat sheet को press brake या bending machine की मदद से मोड़कर required shape, angle और dimensions में बदला जाता है।

Common Sheet Metal Parameters

ParameterMeaning
Thickness (T)Sheet की मोटाई
Inside Radius (R)Bend के अंदर की radius
Bend Angle (A)Sheet की direction में हुआ angular change
K-Factor (K)Thickness के अनुसार neutral axis की position
Bend Allowance (BA)Bend area में neutral axis की developed length
Bend Deduction (BD)Outside dimensions से flat length निकालने के लिए घटाई जाने वाली value
Important: इस guide में bend angle का मतलब sheet की direction में change है। उदाहरण के लिए, 90° bend का मतलब 90° का direction change है। यह अंदर का included angle नहीं है।

2. Inside Bend Radius

Inside bend radius is the radius measured on the inner surface of the bent sheet.

हिंदी में: जब sheet bend होती है, तो bend के अंदर जो गोलाई बनती है उसे Inside Radius कहते हैं।

Illustrative bend-radius diagram; not to scale.

Factors Affecting Inside Radius

  • Material type and grade.
  • Sheet thickness.
  • Punch nose radius.
  • V-die opening.
  • Bending method and machine setup.
In air bending, the actual inside radius depends on material, thickness, tooling and process conditions. Do not assume that the punch radius or V-die opening alone guarantees the final radius.

3. Bend Allowance (BA)

Bend Allowance is the length of the neutral axis along the bend. It represents the material length consumed in the curved bend area.

हिंदी में: Bend के curved portion में neutral axis जितनी length लेती है, उसे Bend Allowance कहते हैं। Flat pattern निकालने में इसका उपयोग होता है।
BA = (π / 180) × A × (R + K × T) A = Bend angle in degrees

Required Inputs

  • A = Bend angle in degrees
  • R = Inside bend radius
  • T = Sheet thickness
  • K = K-Factor

Practical Example

Given:

  • Material: Mild Steel (MS)
  • Thickness (T): 2 mm
  • Inside Radius (R): 3 mm
  • K-Factor: 0.33
  • Bend Angle (A): 90°

Calculation:

BA = (3.1416 / 180) × 90 × (3 + 0.33 × 2)

BA = 1.5708 × (3 + 0.66)

Bend Allowance = 5.75 mm (approx.)

BA is calculated along the neutral axis. The correct K-Factor should be determined from material and actual bending conditions.

4. What is K-Factor?

K-Factor is the ratio of the distance from the inside surface to the neutral axis divided by the sheet thickness.

K = tn / T tₙ = Distance from inside surface to neutral axis
हिंदी में: Bending के समय sheet के अंदर का material compress होता है और बाहर का material stretch होता है। इनके बीच एक neutral axis होती है। K-Factor बताता है कि neutral axis, अंदर की surface से thickness के कितने हिस्से पर है।

Schematic explanation of neutral axis and K-Factor.

Important Points

  • K-Factor is not the same for every material and bending setup.
  • It depends on material, thickness, radius and bending process.
  • Use a verified value from your process, tooling trials or validated calculator.
Do not treat a single K-Factor value as universal. A wrong K-Factor can cause incorrect flat pattern dimensions.

5. Outside Setback (OSSB)

Outside Setback is the distance from the theoretical sharp corner to the bend tangent point, measured along the outside flange.

हिंदी में: अगर bend को एक sharp corner मानें, तो उस काल्पनिक corner से bend के tangent point तक की दूरी Outside Setback कहलाती है।
OSSB = (R + T) × tan(A / 2) A = Bend angle / direction change

Example: 90° Bend

Inside Radius = 3 mm

Thickness = 2 mm

Angle = 90°

OSSB = (3 + 2) × tan(45°)

Outside Setback = 5 mm

6. Bend Deduction (BD)

Bend Deduction is the amount subtracted from the sum of outside flange dimensions to obtain the flat pattern length.

हिंदी में: सभी outside flange dimensions को जोड़ने के बाद flat length निकालने के लिए जितनी value घटाई जाती है, उसे Bend Deduction कहते हैं।
BD = 2 × OSSB − BA

Practical Example

Inside Radius = 3 mm

Thickness = 2 mm

K-Factor = 0.33

Bend Angle = 90°

OSSB = 5 mm

BA = 5.75 mm (approx.)

BD = (2 × 5) − 5.75

Bend Deduction = 4.25 mm (approx.)

7. Flat Pattern / Developed Length

Flat pattern is the required length of the sheet before bending. For a part dimensioned using outside flange dimensions, the bend deductions are subtracted from their total.

हिंदी में: Bending से पहले sheet को जिस flat length में काटना होता है, उसे Developed Length या Flat Pattern Length कहते हैं।
Flat Length = Sum of Outside Dimensions − Sum of Bend Deductions

Example: Two Flanges with One 90° Bend

Outside Dimension 1 = 100 mm

Outside Dimension 2 = 100 mm

Bend Deduction = 4.25 mm

Flat Length = 100 + 100 − 4.25

Flat Length = 195.75 mm

This formula assumes the supplied dimensions are outside dimensions measured to the theoretical sharp corner. If dimensions are given to tangent points, inside surfaces or by another drawing convention, the calculation must be adjusted.

8. Multiple Bend Calculation

When a component has more than one bend, calculate the bend deduction for each bend and subtract their total from the sum of the outside dimensions.

Flat Length = Total Outside Dimensions − (BD₁ + BD₂ + BD₃ + ...)

Example: Three Flanges and Two 90° Bends

Outside dimensions = 50 + 100 + 50 mm

BD for Bend 1 = 4.25 mm

BD for Bend 2 = 4.25 mm

Flat Length = 200 − (4.25 + 4.25)

Flat Length = 191.50 mm

हिंदी में: अगर component में दो या अधिक bends हैं, तो हर bend का BD अलग निकालें। फिर सभी BD को जोड़कर total outside dimensions से घटाएँ।
Different bends may have different radii, angles or process conditions. Do not use one BD value for every bend unless the bend conditions are actually the same.

9. U-Channel and C-Channel Development

U-Channel and C-Channel parts are made by bending a flat blank at multiple locations. Their developed length depends on the straight flange dimensions and the bend deductions.

Illustrative U-channel cross section.

Basic Development Method

  1. Read the drawing and identify all outside dimensions.
  2. Identify the number of bends.
  3. Confirm inside radius and sheet thickness for every bend.
  4. Calculate BA, OSSB and BD for each bend.
  5. Add the outside dimensions.
  6. Subtract the total bend deductions.
  7. Verify the blank by trial bending and inspection.
Developed Length = Total Outside Dimensions − Total BD
हिंदी में: U-Channel या C-Channel की development में सभी flanges और base की drawing dimensions को सही तरीके से पहचानना जरूरी है। हर bend का BD निकालकर total dimensions से घटाएँ।

10. Sheet Metal Bending Process

Typical Press Brake Workflow

  1. Read the engineering drawing.
  2. Confirm material grade and thickness.
  3. Confirm punch and die selection.
  4. Check required inside bend radius.
  5. Calculate flat pattern dimensions.
  6. Mark bend lines on the blank.
  7. Set the machine and back gauge.
  8. Perform a trial bend.
  9. Inspect angle, radius and flange dimensions.
  10. Adjust the process if required and approve the final part.
हिंदी में: पहले drawing और material verify करें। फिर tooling select करके flat pattern और bend line निकालें। Trial bend के बाद angle, radius और dimensions check करें। सही result मिलने पर production शुरू करें।

12. Sheet Metal Inspection Checklist

Use this checklist during first-piece or process inspection.

  • Material grade verified.
  • Sheet thickness checked.
  • Blank size checked.
  • Bend angle checked.
  • Inside radius checked.
  • Flange dimensions checked.
  • Overall dimensions checked.
  • Part orientation and bend direction checked.
  • Drawing revision verified.
  • Inspection results recorded.
Always follow the approved engineering drawing, customer specifications and applicable inspection requirements.

Important Engineering Note

The formulas in this guide are standard geometric calculation methods for common sheet metal bends. Actual production results can vary because of material properties, tooling, machine setup, springback and bending method.

हिंदी में: Formula से मिलने वाला result calculation के लिए है। Actual production में material, tooling, machine setting और springback के कारण थोड़ा अंतर हो सकता है। इसलिए trial bend और inspection जरूरी हैं।
