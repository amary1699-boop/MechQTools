How to Use

  1. Select material.
  2. Enter sheet thickness.
  3. Enter K-factor.
  4. Enter the number of bends.
  5. Click Update Bend Rows.
  6. Enter angle and radius for every bend.
  7. Select dimension method.
  8. Enter profile dimensions.
  9. Click Calculate Development.

Calculation Formula

Bend Allowance
BA = (π / 180) × θ × (R + K × T)
Outside Setback
OSSB = (R + T) × tan(θ / 2)
Bend Deduction
BD = 2 × OSSB − BA

T = Thickness | R = Inside Radius | K = K-Factor
