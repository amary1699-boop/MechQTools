Calculation Process

Enter dimensions and bend details to calculate the flat pattern.

Formula Used

1. Bend Allowance (BA)
BA = (π / 180) × Bend Angle × (R + K × T)

2. Outside Setback (OSSB)
OSSB = (R + T) × tan(Bend Angle / 2)

3. Bend Deduction (BD)
BD = 2 × OSSB − BA

4. Flat Pattern Length
Flat Length = Total Outside Dimensions − Total Bend Deduction

T = Thickness, R = Inside Radius, K = K-Factor. This calculator uses the conventional outside-dimension bend-deduction method.
