Bend Allowance = (π / 180) × Angle × (Radius + K × Thickness)
Total Bend Allowance = Bend Allowance × Number of Bends
Approx. Developed Length = Finished Length + Total Bend Allowance
Important: This calculator provides an approximate development length using the entered K-Factor. Actual sheet-metal development can vary depending on material, forming method, tooling, inside radius, springback and machine setup.
