Formula Used

Bend Allowance (BA)
BA = (π / 180) × Angle × (R + K × T)

Outside Setback (OSSB)
OSSB = (R + T) × tan(Angle / 2)

Bend Deduction (BD)
BD = 2 × OSSB − BA

Flat Length
Flat Length = Total Outside Dimensions − Total BD
