Example Calculation

For an M6 × 1.0 thread with 1.5 × diameter thread engagement:

Thread engagement:
6 × 1.5 = 9 mm

With 2 mm additional safety allowance:
9 + 2 = 11 mm

So the approximate recommended blind-hole depth is 11 mm.

Important Note

Thread depth depends on bolt diameter, material strength, thread class, load, tapping method and application. The value calculated here is a general engineering estimate. For critical components, always follow the drawing, engineering specification, thread standard or manufacturer recommendation. Also remember that the required drill depth can be greater than the final thread depth because of the tap chamfer and chip clearance.
