About This Engineering Unit Converter

This tool is designed for common Mechanical and Quality Engineering calculations.

  • Length conversion between mm, cm, m, inch and ft.
  • Weight / mass conversion between g, kg, ton and lb.
  • Pressure conversion between Pa, kPa, MPa, bar and psi.
  • Force conversion between N, kN and kgf.
  • Temperature conversion between Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin.
Important: For production, inspection and design work, always use the unit and conversion requirements specified by the applicable drawing, standard or manufacturer.
