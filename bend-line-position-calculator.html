Calculation Method

For each bend:
Angle in radians = Bend Angle × π / 180
BA = Angle in radians × (R + K × T)
Setback = (R + T) × tan(Angle / 2)
BD = 2 × Setback − BA

Bend line position:
Xᵢ = (sum of flange dimensions up to bend i) − (sum of previous bend deductions) − (current bend deduction / 2)

Total flat pattern length:
Flat Length = Sum of all flange dimensions − Sum of all bend deductions
Important: These formulas assume outside dimensions between theoretical sharp corners and the entered angle is the bend rotation angle. Direction (Up/Down) changes the profile orientation, not the flat development calculation. Confirm the dimension convention and angle convention against your drawing and press-brake setup before production use.
