How It Works

  • Cutting Speed (Vc) is the surface speed between the cutting tool and workpiece.
  • RPM means spindle revolutions per minute.
  • For drilling, RPM depends on cutting speed and drill diameter.
  • For milling, feed rate depends on RPM, number of teeth and feed per tooth.
Drilling Example

Cutting Speed = 25 m/min
Drill Diameter = 10 mm

RPM ≈ 796 RPM

Formula:
RPM = (1000 × Vc) ÷ (π × D)
