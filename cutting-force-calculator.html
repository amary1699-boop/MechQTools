Formula:

Cutting Force:
Fc = kc × A

Where:
Fc = Cutting force in N
kc = Specific cutting force in N/mm²
A = Effective cutting area in mm²

Example Calculation

For turning:

  • Specific cutting force = 1800 N/mm²
  • Depth of cut = 2 mm
  • Feed = 0.20 mm/rev

Cutting area:
2 × 0.20 = 0.40 mm²

Cutting force:
1800 × 0.40 = 720 N

Therefore:
Approximate cutting force = 0.72 kN

Important Note

This calculator provides an approximate cutting-force estimate. Actual cutting force depends on:
  • Workpiece material
  • Tool material and geometry
  • Rake angle
  • Cutting speed
  • Feed
  • Depth of cut
  • Tool wear
  • Coolant and cutting condition
The specific cutting force kc should ideally be taken from the material/tool manufacturer's machining data.
