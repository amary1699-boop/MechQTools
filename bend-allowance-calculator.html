What is Bend Allowance?

Bend allowance is the amount of material required in the neutral axis of a sheet metal part during bending. It is used when calculating the flat pattern or developed length of a bent sheet metal component.

Bend Allowance Formula

The basic bend allowance formula used by this calculator is:

BA = (π / 180) × A × (R + K × T)

Where:
BA = Bend Allowance
A = Bend Angle in degrees
R = Inside Bend Radius
K = K-Factor
T = Material Thickness

Example Calculation

Suppose the sheet metal parameters are:

  • Material thickness = 2 mm
  • Inside bend radius = 3 mm
  • K-factor = 0.33
  • Bend angle = 90°
BA = (π / 180) × 90 × (3 + 0.33 × 2)

BA ≈ 5.75 mm

Why is K-Factor Important?

The K-factor represents the position of the neutral axis within the sheet thickness during bending. Different materials, tooling, bend radii and manufacturing processes can result in different K-factor values.

Where is Bend Allowance Used?

  • Sheet metal flat pattern development
  • Press brake bending
  • Fabrication drawings
  • Manufacturing calculations
  • CAD sheet metal development
  • Laser cutting and bending jobs

Important Note

The actual bend allowance can vary depending on material, tooling, bending method, grain direction and manufacturing conditions. Always verify the calculated value with your manufacturing process or shop-floor bend test when accuracy is critical.
