Bend Allowance Calculator

Calculate sheet metal bend allowance for any bend angle using material thickness, inside bend radius and K-factor.

Result

0.000 mm
Formula:
BA = (π / 180) × Bend Angle × (R + K × T)

How to use

  1. Enter material thickness.
  2. Enter inside bend radius.
  3. Enter K-Factor.
  4. Enter any bend angle from 1° to 179.9°.
  5. Click Calculate Bend Allowance.

Example: T = 2 mm, R = 3 mm, K = 0.33 and angle = 90° gives approximately 5.75 mm.
