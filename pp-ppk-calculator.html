Pp Formula:
Pp = (USL − LSL) ÷ (6 × Overall Standard Deviation)

Ppk Formula:
Ppk = Min[ (USL − Mean) ÷ (3 × SD), (Mean − LSL) ÷ (3 × SD) ]
Important: Pp and Ppk are process performance indices based on overall/long-term process variation. Pp mainly evaluates the overall process spread, while Ppk also considers how well the process mean is centered between the specification limits. Actual quality requirements should always follow the drawing, customer specification and applicable standard.
