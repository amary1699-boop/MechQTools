Manufacturing Process Knowledge

Practical knowledge of Fabrication, Welding and Painting for Mechanical and Quality Engineers.

About This Guide

This guide covers common manufacturing processes and their quality inspection requirements. It is useful for Quality Engineers, Production Engineers and Mechanical Engineers.

Hindi: Is guide mein fabrication, welding aur painting ki basic se practical knowledge di gayi hai. Saath mein defects, inspection points aur interview questions bhi hain.

1. Fabrication Knowledge

What is Fabrication?

Fabrication is the process of converting raw material such as sheet, plate, pipe and structural sections into a finished component or assembly through cutting, forming, fitting and joining.

Hindi: Raw material ko cutting, bending, forming, fitting aur joining karke required component banana fabrication hai.

Cutting

Shearing, laser cutting, plasma cutting, gas cutting and sawing.

Check: Length, width, profile, burr and edge quality.

Bending

Press brake bending, V-bending, U-bending and air bending.

Check: Angle, flange length, radius and springback.

Rolling

Sheet or plate is passed through rollers to make curved shapes.

Check: Diameter, radius, roundness and straightness.

Drilling

Making holes using a drill machine or CNC equipment.

Check: Hole diameter, location, burr and pitch.

Fit-up

Positioning and aligning components before welding.

Check: Root gap, alignment, squareness and tack weld.

Grinding

Removing sharp edges, excess weld metal or surface irregularities.

Check: Surface damage, undercut and excessive material removal.

Fabrication Inspection Checklist

  • Verify material grade and thickness.
  • Check drawing number and revision.
  • Inspect cutting dimensions and profile.
  • Check bend angle, radius and flange dimensions.
  • Verify hole size and hole location.
  • Check assembly alignment and squareness.
  • Inspect sharp edges, cracks and visible damage.
  • Record inspection results.

2. Welding Knowledge

What is Welding?

Welding is a joining process in which two or more materials are joined using heat, pressure or both, with or without filler material.

Hindi: Heat, pressure ya dono ki help se do metal parts ko permanently join karna welding hai.

Arc Welding

Uses an electric arc to generate heat for joining metals.

Common name: SMAW / Manual Metal Arc.

MIG / MAG

Uses a continuously fed wire electrode and shielding gas.

Use: Common fabrication and production work.

TIG Welding

Uses a non-consumable tungsten electrode.

Use: Controlled, clean welding applications.

Spot Welding

Resistance welding that joins overlapping sheet metal at points.

Check: Weld nugget and joint requirements.

Submerged Arc

Arc is covered by granular flux during welding.

Use: Suitable heavy fabrication applications.

Gas Welding

Uses a fuel gas and oxygen flame as the heat source.

Use: Selected repair and joining applications.

Welding Quality Inspection Checklist

  • Verify approved drawing and weld symbols.
  • Check material identification and joint preparation.
  • Verify fit-up and root gap as specified.
  • Check approved WPS and qualified welder requirements.
  • Inspect weld size, length and location.
  • Check visible defects and weld profile.
  • Perform required NDT as per inspection plan.
  • Verify dimensional distortion after welding.
  • Maintain inspection and traceability records.

3. Painting Knowledge

What is Industrial Painting?

Industrial painting is a protective coating process used to protect metal surfaces against corrosion, chemicals, moisture and environmental exposure, while also providing the required finish.

Hindi: Metal surface ko rust aur environment se protect karne ke liye coating lagane ki process industrial painting hai.

Surface Preparation

Cleaning, degreasing, grinding and removal of contamination.

Check: Surface cleanliness and preparation grade.

Shot Blasting

Abrasive particles are used to clean and prepare metal surfaces.

Check: Surface cleanliness and profile.

Primer

First coating layer that supports adhesion and corrosion protection.

Check: Product, mixing and specified thickness.

Intermediate Coat

Additional coating layer used in specified paint systems.

Check: Recoat interval and thickness.

Top Coat

Final coating that provides appearance and specified protection.

Check: Colour, finish and DFT.

Curing

Coating develops its required properties over the specified time.

Check: Curing conditions and time.

Painting Inspection Checklist

  • Verify approved paint specification and coating system.
  • Check surface cleanliness and preparation.
  • Verify environmental conditions as specified.
  • Check paint batch, shelf life and mixing instructions.
  • Verify recoat interval and curing requirements.
  • Measure DFT at required locations.
  • Inspect colour, finish, sagging, pinholes and peeling.
  • Perform adhesion or other specified tests when required.
  • Record results and maintain traceability.

4. Common Quality Engineer Interview Questions

Q1. What is fabrication?

Answer: Fabrication is the process of making components through cutting, forming, fitting and joining.

Hindi: Cutting, bending, fitting aur joining se component banana fabrication hai.

Q2. What is the difference between MIG and TIG welding?

Answer: MIG uses a continuously fed consumable wire electrode. TIG uses a non-consumable tungsten electrode and may use separate filler material.

Hindi: MIG mein wire electrode continuously feed hota hai, jabki TIG mein tungsten electrode use hota hai.

Q3. What is WPS?

Answer: WPS means Welding Procedure Specification. It defines the approved welding variables and instructions for a welding operation.

Hindi: WPS batata hai ki approved procedure ke according welding kaise karni hai.

Q4. What is porosity?

Answer: Porosity is the presence of gas cavities in weld metal.

Hindi: Weld ke andar gas ke chhote cavities ya holes hona porosity hai.

Q5. What is DFT?

Answer: DFT means Dry Film Thickness. It is the thickness of the coating after drying or curing, measured as specified.

Hindi: Paint dry hone ke baad coating ki thickness DFT hoti hai.

Q6. How do you inspect a welded component?

Answer: Review the drawing and WPS requirements, check fit-up, weld size and location, perform visual inspection and arrange specified NDT if required.

Hindi: Drawing, weld size, location, visible defects aur required NDT check karenge.

Q7. How do you inspect a painted component?

Answer: Check surface preparation, coating specification, DFT, colour, finish, curing and specified tests.

Hindi: Surface preparation, DFT, colour, finish aur paint defects check karenge.

Q8. What will you do if a defect is found?

Answer: Identify and record the defect, segregate or control the nonconforming product, inform the responsible team, follow the approved disposition and verify corrective action.

Hindi: Defect record karenge, part ko control karenge, concerned team ko inform karenge aur approved correction ke baad re-inspection karenge.
Important Engineering Note: Actual acceptance criteria, weld size, NDT method, paint DFT, surface preparation grade and testing frequency must follow the approved engineering drawing, applicable standard, customer specification and inspection plan. This guide is for learning; it does not replace approved production requirements.
