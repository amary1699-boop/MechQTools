Common Torque Coefficient Values

Condition Approx. K Typical Use

Important Note

Bolt torque calculated here is an approximate value. Actual tightening torque depends on:
  • Bolt grade and material
  • Lubrication condition
  • Thread friction
  • Under-head friction
  • Washer condition
  • Required preload
  • Manufacturer specification
For critical applications, always use the specified torque from the drawing, standard, fastener manufacturer or assembly specification.
