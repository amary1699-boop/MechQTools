How to Use

This calculator compares the measured GD&T deviation with the specified tolerance.

  • Select the required GD&T feature.
  • Enter the measured deviation in mm.
  • Enter the allowed GD&T tolerance in mm.
  • Click Calculate.
  • The calculator will show the remaining tolerance and Pass/Fail result.

Example

Suppose the specified flatness tolerance is 0.050 mm and the measured flatness deviation is 0.030 mm.

Remaining tolerance = 0.050 − 0.030 = 0.020 mm

Because the measured deviation is within the specified tolerance, the result is PASS.
