PPAP Elements

1. Design Records
Approved engineering drawings, specifications and design documents.
2. Engineering Change Documents
Approved engineering change documents, if applicable.
3. Customer Engineering Approval
Customer engineering approval or evidence of approval.
4. Design FMEA
Design Failure Mode and Effects Analysis, when applicable.
5. Process Flow Diagram
Complete process flow showing all manufacturing operations.
6. Process FMEA
Process Failure Mode and Effects Analysis for manufacturing processes.
7. Control Plan
Control plan covering process controls, inspections and reactions.
8. Measurement System Analysis
MSA studies such as Gauge R&R and measurement system validation.
9. Dimensional Results
Dimensional inspection results against approved drawing requirements.
10. Material / Performance Test Results
Material certificates and required performance or laboratory test results.
11. Initial Process Studies
Initial process capability studies such as Cp and Cpk, when required.
12. Qualified Laboratory Documentation
Laboratory accreditation or qualification documentation where applicable.
13. Appearance Approval Report
Appearance Approval Report for applicable appearance items.
14. Sample Production Parts
Production representative samples submitted for approval.
15. Master Sample
Master sample retained and identified as required.
16. Checking Aids
Inspection fixtures, gauges and checking aids used for verification.
17. Customer-Specific Requirements
Applicable customer-specific requirements and supporting documents.
18. Part Submission Warrant
PSW completed, reviewed and submitted for customer approval.
