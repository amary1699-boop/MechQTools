Calculation Formulas:

Angle in Radians = Bend Angle × π / 180

Bend Allowance (BA) = Angle in Radians × (R + K × T)

Setback = (R + T) × tan(Bend Angle / 2)

Bend Deduction (BD) = 2 × Setback − BA

How to Use

  1. Select Material Type.
  2. Enter Material Thickness.
  3. Enter Inside Bend Radius.
  4. Enter Bend Angle.
  5. Enter K-Factor, or use the default.
  6. Enter BA manually or receive it from Bend Allowance Calculator.
  7. Click Calculate Bend Deduction.

BA Calculation Options

Option 1 — Automatic:

Leave the BA field empty. The calculator calculates BA from K-Factor.

Option 2 — Manual / Transferred:

Enter BA yourself or receive it using the Use in Bend Deduction button in the Bend Allowance Calculator.

Important Engineering Notes

Material and K-Factor:

This calculator supports MS, SS, Aluminium, GI, Copper, Brass and other materials. Material selection identifies the material but does not automatically determine its exact K-Factor. K-Factor depends on material properties, thickness, inside radius, tooling and bending method.

The default K-Factor of 0.33 is only a starting assumption. Use a verified K-Factor or a measured Bend Allowance for production accuracy.

Large inside radii are accepted by the calculator. However, verify the actual bend geometry and process data before using results for production.
