Formulas:

Setback = tan(Bend Angle / 2) × (R + T)

Bend Deduction = 2 × Setback − Bend Allowance

Flat Pattern Length = A + B − Bend Deduction
Important:
This calculator assumes that A and B are outside dimensions measured to the theoretical sharp corner. The calculation is intended for standard sheet-metal bend calculations and should be verified with your actual machine, tooling, material and shop-floor bend data before production use.

How to use

  1. Enter Outside Dimension A.
  2. Enter Outside Dimension B.
  3. Enter Material Thickness.
  4. Enter Inside Bend Radius.
  5. Enter Bend Angle.
  6. Enter the Bend Allowance for the same material and bend condition.
  7. Click Calculate Bend Deduction.

Example

A = 100 mm
B = 100 mm
T = 2 mm
R = 3 mm
Bend Angle = 90°
BA = 5.75 mm

For this example, the Bend Deduction is approximately 4.250 mm and the flat pattern length is approximately 195.750 mm.
