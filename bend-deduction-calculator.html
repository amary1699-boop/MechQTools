Bend Deduction Calculator

Calculate Sheet Metal Bend Deduction using material thickness, inside radius, bend angle and K-Factor.

Typical starting value: 0.33

Calculation Formulas:

Angle in Radians = Bend Angle × π / 180

Bend Allowance (BA) = Angle in Radians × (R + K × T)

Setback = (R + T) × tan(Bend Angle / 2)

Bend Deduction (BD) = 2 × Setback − Bend Allowance

How to Use

  1. Enter Material Thickness (T).
  2. Enter Inside Bend Radius (R).
  3. Enter Bend Angle in degrees.
  4. Enter K-Factor (default 0.33).
  5. Click Calculate.
  6. Check the Bend Deduction result.

Calculation Example

Thickness (T) = 2 mm
Inside Radius (R) = 3 mm
Bend Angle = 90°
K-Factor = 0.33

Bend Allowance ≈ 5.749 mm
Setback = 5.000 mm

Bend Deduction ≈ 4.251 mm

Important:

K-Factor depends on material, thickness, inside radius, tooling and bending process. The default value 0.33 is only a starting assumption. For accurate production results, use a K-Factor established from your actual material and machine bending data.

This calculator uses the entered bend angle as the included bend angle in the formulas. Confirm the angle convention used by your drawing and shop-floor process.
